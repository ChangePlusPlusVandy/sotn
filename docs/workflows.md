## **Notes**

### **Platform Direction**

* Centralized recruitment, intake, and participant-routing platform for Train4Life and Special Olympics Tennessee  
* Potential to expand beyond T4L and support other Special Olympics programs in the future  
* Serves as an entry point into the broader Special Olympics ecosystem  
* Connects users to appropriate programs, resources, staff, and external systems  
* Future integration possibilities include COE and other operational platforms through APIs

### **User Groups**

* Athletes (with or without IDD)  
* Guardians/caregivers  
* Volunteers  
* Coaches  
* Schools and venues  
* Donors, sponsors, and community partners  
* Administrative users

### **Platform Structure**

* Two-sided platform  
  * User-facing onboarding and intake experience  
  * Administrative dashboard for management and routing  
* User-facing side focused on onboarding, qualification, and education  
* Administrative side focused on participant management, communication, and routing workflows

### **Onboarding and Qualification Flow**

* User enters platform  
* User submits information  
* Platform evaluates interests, qualifications, and program fit  
* User receives resources and next-step guidance  
* Information is routed to appropriate staff and programs  
* Human review step included to prevent spam, malicious submissions, and edge cases

### **Information Routing**

* Route information to state staff first  
* State staff determine appropriate program ownership  
* Information may then be routed to department heads, program leads, regional leads, or area directors as needed  
* Avoid placing excessive administrative burden directly on volunteers

### **Communication and Follow-Up**

* Automated next-step communications after qualification  
* One-click methods for expressing interest in programs  
* Automated distribution of onboarding information and resources  
* Follow-up process for users who may not initially qualify for a program  
* Ability to retain and revisit user information for future opportunities

### **Administrative Dashboard**

Potential functionality discussed:

* Participant routing  
* Contact management  
* Communication tracking  
* Notes and meeting summaries  
* Priority lists  
* Follow-up reminders  
* Notification system  
* Intake oversight

### **Design Direction**

* Clean, organized, and accessible interface  
* Modern visual identity while maintaining alignment with Special Olympics branding  
* More visually engaging than existing Special Olympics systems  
* Strong emphasis on accessibility and user experience  
* Corporate and management-focused design for administrative dashboard
